# Light Block A5 — editable electronics checkpoint

This directory contains **three native KiCad 10 projects with complete schematics and part-to-pin connectivity**. The identical LED leaf and passive power adapter are routed and pass native PCB DRC. The controller contains the selected components and fixed interfaces, but **has no copper routing or planes and is not a manufacturing file**.

No hardware was assembled or tested during this work. Native software checks, ideal-circuit calculations, mechanical design intentions and physical qualification are kept distinct.

## What changed electrically from A4.1

The A4.1 passive CV-through path, 20 MΩ pickup, OPA197 buffer, 82 kΩ affine network, MCU and independent left/right LED data channels remain the circuit basis. A5 adds **U7 TLV9001IDBVR / C398363**, **R24 330 Ω / C23138**, **R25 1 kΩ / C21190** and **C17 100 nF / C14663**. These add four fitted pieces and one manufacturer type. A later mechanical correction removes controller J4 and adds a second SH4 to each identical leaf, a net **one additional C160404 connector**. Both original 50 mm completed SH4 cables remain in use.

U7 buffers the affine input before the MCU ADC. R24 and R25 form a nominal 100/133 divider and provide a defined low impedance to ground at the ADC. The input capacitor C13 is now **upstream of the existing R7**, on `CV_AFFINE`. R7 connects that filtered node to `CV_FILTER`, where the BAS70 clamp and U7 input connect. Thus the 10 nF capacitor cannot discharge directly into the U7 input clamp during a supply collapse. This uses the existing resistor and does not add R26.

The normal DC relationship is:

\[
V_{ADC}=\frac{100}{133}\left(\frac{V_{DD}}{2}+\frac{V_{CV}}{8}\right),
\qquad V_{CV}=10.64V_{ADC}-4V_{DD}.
\]

At 3.3 V, the nominal ADC values for −5 / 0 / +5 V are **0.77068 / 1.24060 / 1.71053 V**. The ideal affine network has a 20.5 kΩ Thevenin resistance, so the relocated 10 nF capacitor gives a nominal **776.37 Hz** pole. Firmware uses this A5 transfer explicitly; A4.1's unbuffered conversion settings must not be reused.

`reports/electrical_margins.json` is regenerated by `design/check_electrical_margins.py`. It checks 256 resistor combinations and two supply endpoints for each CV test input. This is an ideal resistor calculation, excluding op-amp error, ADC/reference error, leakage, temperature coefficients and ageing.

### Scope of the power-off improvement

Two bounded calculations are useful, but neither is a blanket power-sequencing approval:

* With CV in ±5 V, initial VDD between 3.0 and 3.6 V, and 1% resistor corners, maximum C13 precharge is about **2.44335 V**. R7 is at least 990 Ω. If its receiving node remains at or above −0.5 V, that capacitor path is limited to **2.974 mA**, below the TLV9001's stated 10 mA over-rail input-current condition.
* For a steady-DC scenario with no other stored or supplied energy reaching the ADC, a deliberately conservative `|CV_BUF| ≤ 20 V` source through R3 ≥81.18 kΩ supplies at most 246.37 µA. Assigning all of it to R25 ≤1.01 kΩ gives **|VADC| ≤0.24883 V**; including R7 tightens the bound. This does not assume that an unpowered TLV9001 has a specified output voltage.

The remaining gates are explicit: **partial supplies, U7's differential-input absolute maximum, all transient energy paths, regulator reverse current, ESD and rail overshoot have not been qualified**. The 1 kΩ shunts and revised capacitor placement improve defined paths; they do not establish unspecified semiconductor behavior. TI specifies the TLV9001's over-rail current condition and a separate differential-input limit; both matter. ST's ADC voltage limits cannot be replaced by an injection-current-only argument. Sources: [TI TLV9001](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9001.pdf), [ST STM32G031](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32g031f8.pdf).

## Fixed hardware interfaces

| STM32 UFQFPN28 pin | Signal | Behavior |
|---:|---|---|
| 3 / 4 | VDD / VSS | 3.3 V / ground |
| 5 | PF2 / NRST | 10 kΩ pull-up and 100 nF to ground |
| 6 | PA0 / ADC_IN0 | Buffered and divided CV input |
| 8 / 9 | PA2 / PA3 | Active-low switch throws, external 10 kΩ pull-ups |
| 10 | PA4 | Active-low LED `/FAULT` |
| 11 | PA5 | **High enables** VLED through the NPN |
| 12 / 13 | PA6 / PA7 | AF1 TIM3 CH1 / CH2, independent LED streams |
| 20 / 21 | PA13 / PA14 | SWDIO / SWCLK |

Switch common pin 2 is grounded. `(MODE_A, MODE_B)` is 01 ON, 11 PROGRAM, 10 CV, 00 invalid. The actual installed lever direction still requires the incoming switch continuity check.

The SWD row is on the **rear face at local Y32.2 / global Y80.7**. In increasing PCB X, it is **3V3 sense, GND, SWDIO, SWCLK, NRST**. The probe must not back-power the board. Removing the second controller header freed this access area and removed the earlier switch-body obstruction. Fixture approach and optical/harness removal still require mechanical qualification; it is not a claim of unobstructed field access in a fully assembled module.

**CV IN and THRU are passive copper connections in the schematic.** PJ-3582 pin 6 is the tip; both tips share `CV_THRU`. Pin 2 is sleeve and pin 3 ring, both grounded for this mono CV use. Insertion-detect contacts 4 and 5 are unused. Connector drawing interpretation is independently checked, but incoming contact continuity remains a factory gate. The controller PCB must still route that passive connection.

**Both independent LED streams share one controller SH4 and continue through the identical leaves.** They are not serialized into one eight-LED chain. The cables are straight 1:1 and use this exact mapping:

| Physical connector | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 | Pin 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Controller J3 | GND | VLED | DATA_L | DATA_R |
| Left leaf J1 | GND | VLED | Left leaf DIN | Right stream through |
| Left leaf J2 | GND | VLED | Right stream through | Left stream stub |
| Right leaf J1 | GND | VLED | Right leaf DIN | Left stream stub |
| Right leaf J2, unused externally | GND | VLED | Left stream stub | Right stream stub |

Cable 1 connects controller J3 to left J1. Cable 2 connects left J2 to right J1. On either identical leaf, J1 pins 3/4 are `DATA_IN` / `DATA_OTHER`, and J2 swaps them. Pin 4 is active in this revision. This is a custom assignment, not a Qwiic interface. The right board is flipped end-for-end, reversing its physical pixel order. Uniform-per-chain firmware is unaffected; future spatial patterns must account for this mapping.

The first SH4 cable now carries both leaves' current. JST specifies 1.0 A with AWG28, versus the TPS limit maximum of about 0.439 A. The linked Adafruit drawing specifies AWG28; current delivered construction remains an incoming-lot check. At that current, an ideal 35 µm copper calculation for the first leaf's 0.5 mm rails and 0.35 mm connector branches gives about **59 mV loop drop and 26 mW loss** through the bridge path. This excludes contacts, cable resistance, copper tolerance, temperature and transients; it is not a thermal qualification. Sources: [JST SH](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eSH.pdf), [Adafruit linked cable drawing](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/4399/C14172-001_Kaweei_CBH-100-04-050-A.pdf).

The power cable is Molex 151340300, whose endpoints reverse pins 1 and 3. Accordingly, controller J5 is **1 +12 / 2 GND / 3 −12**, while adapter J2 is **1 −12 / 2 GND / 3 +12**. The IDC connector is **pins 1–2 −12 (red stripe), 3–8 GND, 9–10 +12**. The adapter's copper deliberately compensates the completed cable's reversal.

## Protection and LED behavior

D1's anode faces raw +12 V and its cathode feeds protected +12 V. D2's cathode faces raw −12 V and its anode feeds protected −12 V. These are the intended opposite series-diode polarities for the bipolar supply; actual reverse-connection and partial-rail tests remain required.

The AP63205 produces the unswitched 5 V rail. The TPS2552DBVR-1 is the **active-low, overcurrent latch-off variant**. Its EN pin is pulled up to unswitched 5 V, so VLED defaults off. PA5 high drives an NPN that pulls EN low. The NPN has a base pull-down. The 68 kΩ, 1% ILIM resistor gives the manufacturer-equation range **343.3–439.4 mA**, nominal **388.1 mA**. This is an electrical protection threshold, not a permissible enclosure heat load.

Toggling EN or removing input power clears the hardware overcurrent latch. Firmware therefore records an armed/fault state before enabling and inhibits ordinary warm-reset retries. The TPS family also has thermal behavior that must not be confused with a permanent thermal latch. Firmware brightness/thermal PWM policy remains separate from current protection. Source: [TI TPS2552 family](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2552.pdf).

The SN74LVC2T45 uses VCCA=3.3 V, VCCB=switched VLED, and DIR tied to VCCA. Input and output pull-downs give defined idle levels; the manufacturer's Ioff/isolation behavior supports the intended supply sequencing. Measured startup, shutdown and data behavior still belongs in prototype qualification. Source: [TI SN74LVC2T45](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2t45.pdf).

The exact Worldsemi manufacturer file currently has footer **V1.4**, even though its URL ends `v6-1.0.pdf`. Its T1-low range differs from an older distributor copy. Firmware uses the current table and a 60 MHz timer with 78 ticks per cell, 21 ticks high for zero and 57 for one, with a 320 µs reset tail. Verify the incoming LED lot and the actual waveform. The chosen LED remains a Standard PCBA part with MSL handling requirements. Source: [Worldsemi WS2812B-2020-V6](https://www.world-semi.com/downloads/datasheets/en/ws2812b-2020-v6-1.0.pdf).

## Mechanical and board details

The controller is 8.8 ×62.9 ×1.6 mm; global Y48.5–111.4, front Z12.6. Its only LED connector J3 has final origin global **Y60.5**, opening toward −Y. Controller J4 is absent. The adapter is 8.8 ×30 ×1.6 mm, global Y18–48, front Z17.4. Its IDC pin-array center is Y33.4 and PicoBlade pin row Y46.7.

Each leaf is **9.0 ×82.5 ×0.6 mm**, with LEDs at local Y3.75, 28.75, 53.75 and 78.75. Both SH4s face +local Y, with origins at Y16.5 and Y66.0. Two symmetric front-edge notches, X0–1 mm and Y23.2–25.2 /57.3–59.3, clear the adapter tail geometry in either flipped orientation. The power rail detours around both notches. Internal-corner cutter radius and board fabrication tolerances require factory review; the mechanical scenario uses a maximum 0.5 mm internal radius.

Some controller courtyard projections are deliberate design intentions, **not DRC exclusions**:

* J3 projects into the rear M2 keepout. The mechanical model puts the nut at Z15.4–17.0, above an insulating PETG clamp, with a local pad pocket. Solder height and clamp clearance remain gates.
* J3's rectangular courtyard intersects C7's courtyard; their nominal molded-body projections are separated by about 0.825 mm. Check the actual mated plug, wire bend and clamp relief, not just library rectangles.

The former J4 mated-plug conflict with the toggle tail and D3 is **resolved in the current topology by removing J4**. The new bridge cable follows the mechanical model around the front of the leaves; its 1.5–1.75 mm design bend radius and actual completed-cable fit remain sample gates. Four resistors and the SWD row now occupy the freed rear area, so no under-switch part placement is required. Switch slots were increased to **1.8 ×1.2 mm with 2.3 ×1.7 mm pads**, providing a nominal 0.25 mm annulus, because the earlier 1.6 ×1.0 mm oval did not contain the rectangular terminal's corners with tolerance.

The controller still needs routing, local bypass optimization, grounded return paths and full power-loop review. The buck input capacitor, switch node, bootstrap loop and feedback return must be treated as a compact power circuit. C3's present placement and the remote bypass/ILIM placements require attention before routing is called final. The selected Samsung MLCCs have nominal capacitance margin, but **effective capacitance versus DC bias, temperature, tolerance and ageing is not proven**. No unsupported minimum-capacitance value is asserted. The TDK inductor is the active `SPM4020T-4R7M-LR`; its typical saturation rating does not by itself close the AP63205 hot-fault requirement. Sources: [Diodes AP63205](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf), [TDK SPM4020-LR](https://product.tdk.com/system/files/dam/doc/product/inductor/inductor/smd/catalog/inductor_commercial_power_spm4020-lr_en.pdf).

## Reproduction and evidence

Open any native `.kicad_pro` with **KiCad 10**. Symbols and footprints are supplied locally; no global library installation is required for editing, ERC or DRC. Optional upstream package 3D models are not bundled; the separate mechanical assembly is the current component-envelope study. KiCad's licensed upstream footprint sources and license are in `footprints/upstream/`. Manufacturer source URLs, document hashes, drawing pages and extracted design facts are listed in `source_provenance.json`; the package does not require redistributed manufacturer PDFs. The historical Würth inductor alternative is not a drop-in claim for this TDK land pattern. The assembly process must also reconcile TDK's no-cleaning guidance with leakage control around the20MΩ CV pickup.

To regenerate with KiCad's Python module available:

```sh
python3 design/generate_electronics.py
./validate_native.sh
```

Set `KICAD_CLI` if the executable is not on PATH. A portable extracted KiCad may additionally require its own per-process library/data/Python paths. Those environment paths are intentionally not hard-coded into the deliverable. `design/place_controller.py` is an exploratory packer that writes a separate experiment file, **not the authoritative placement**; the reviewed overrides are `design/controller_placement_override.json`. Native regeneration can assign new internal UUIDs, so rerun verification and refresh the SHA snapshot afterward.

The authoritative exact-file record is **`reports/pcb_qualification.json`**. It binds each board, project rule file, DRC report and footprint source to SHA-256 hashes. `placement_and_connectivity.json` records exact native pad centers, board sides, component poses and nominal body envelopes; the native boards remain the authority for actual copper geometry.
