# engineering/a7 — A7 structural redesign (study in progress)

A7 is the structural redesign tracked in [#15](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/15):
right-angle threaded CV jacks with a controller PCB perpendicular to the front, a PCB-side ON / PGM / CV
setting switch instead of the front toggle, and the 0.8 mm rigid FR-4 optical redesign in [#87](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/87). Transparent PET/FPC is withdrawn.

**Status: design study. Not released for manufacture.** Nothing here is part approval, fit approval or an
electrical result. `engineering/a5/` stays the immutable A5 checkpoint; A7 never edits it.

Start with [`docs/continuation/continue-here.md`](docs/continuation/continue-here.md) for the current state,
next actions and blockers, and [`docs/continuation/evidence-index.md`](docs/continuation/evidence-index.md) for every evidence set.

## 現行方針: 0.8 mm rigid FR-4 への設計変更（2026-11-17 JST）

親 [#15](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/15) の新しい変更を [#87](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/87) で追跡します。透明PCBをrigid FR-4の代替と考えていましたが、実際は薄いflex PCBだったため、透明PET/FPC案は撤回しました。普通のrigid FR-4基材のくすんだ半透明性を試します。透過率、拡散、実物の見え方は未測定です。

- LED leafとcontrollerは公称 **0.8 mm**。完成板厚の評価範囲は **0.7–0.9 mm**。両面maskは必要箇所以外を開口し、内層も含め広いGND面を撤去します。必要な配線、pad、via、局所銅とmaskの位置・理由を記録し、全銅層と表裏maskのGerberを確認します。
- **2HP（9.8 mm）を優先**して公差・配線・組立条件を評価し、成立根拠を確保できなければ **3HP（14.9 mm）**へ進みます。3HPでも0.8 mm、最小mask、広いGND面なしを維持します。旧「最も厚い成立板」の規則は適用しません。
- 8 RGB LED、左右各4個の独立2stream、共通電流制限VLED、passive CV IN→THRU、MCUのpin/伝達関数/既存mode、前面128.5 mm・cast PMMA 2 mm・奥行30 mm目標、工場実装と完成ケーブルを維持します。
- 新leafの正本はA5外に設けます。旧leaf 0.6 mm、新leaf 0.8 mm、旧controller 1.6 mmを混同しません。leaf積層は0.8×2＋bond 0.1＝**1.70 mm nominal**で、公差・支持・治具・機構・製造入力・プレビューを再検証します。
- この指示は設計方針の承認です。旧PCBのnative PASS、治具PASS、試作ルート承認、`order/2026-11-13/`は旧identityに限る履歴で、新設計に付け替えません。新設計の統合検証と製造releaseは未完了です。

PET供給元への質問は履歴として保管し、現行FR-4案の前提条件・次作業から外します。実機の透過性・熱・電気動作・組立・治具接触・収まりは別の物理作業です。A5は不変、A6 #7 / draft PR #14は保留。発注、supplier連絡、書込み・通電、別deploy、hosting/security設定変更は行いません。

## R1 findings (historical, 2026-11-08)

### Parts

- **All manufacturer documents for both parts are LCSC-hosted mirrors.** The SOFNG drawing and the C&K datasheet
  were obtained only from datasheet.lcsc.com, so they are classed MANUFACTURER_MIRROR. The maker-hosted copies
  were SOURCE UNAVAILABLE on 2026-11-08 (sofng.com: paged lists reset, later HTTP 502; C&K's PDF link redirects
  to Littelfuse, which answered HTTP 403). Every value read from them is kept as UNVERIFIED / UNSOURCED with the
  value retained, and the study labels those inputs MIRROR. Nothing below is primary-source confirmed.
- **Jack: SOFNG PJ-324M, JLCPCB/LCSC C22355739** (`.claude/skills/component-a7-pj-324m/`). The SOFNG drawing (mirror) prints
  M6x0.5, body height 6.0 (headline 6.3), width 11.5 with the axis 5.4 from one edge (so 6.1 from the other, calculated),
  body 14.2 behind a 3.5 barrel, 3.2 untrimmed terminals from the seating plane, and the terminal positions.
  It does **not** dimension the axis height (carried as INFERRED ≈3.0, band 2.6–3.4), give hole sizes, label
  pin functions, or fill in the operating temperature. JLC lists it as a wave-soldered plug-in part; stock 6209
  on 2026-11-08.
- **Pin functions are inferred, not sourced.** Pin 1 = sleeve, 2 = ring, 4 = tip, 3/5 = normals is the
  proposal (symbol convention plus terminal depth). A tip/ring swap would put CV_THRU on the contact that
  touches a TS plug's sleeve, so an incoming-part continuity test with TS and TRS plugs is required.
- **Nut:** no nut is shown or listed with C22355739. Same Sky `3.5mm-nut-e` (Ø8.0 × 1.5) and `MJ-3502-nut`
  (Ø8.0 × 1.8) are M6x0.5 dimension references only. Their fit on the SOFNG barrel is unverified.
- **Side switch: C&K PCM13SMTR, LCSC C221842** (`.claude/skills/component-a7-pcm13smtr/`). The manufacturer table (mirror)
  gives POS.1 = 1-3, POS.2 = 2-3, POS.3 = 3-4 with terminal 3 common. With 3 = GND, 1 = MODE_A, 4 = MODE_B and
  2 open, the positions give ON (0,1), PROGRAM (1,1, physical centre) and CV (1,0), as the A5 firmware expects.
  It is side-actuated (actuator projects 1.5 mm past the body edge, 1.5 mm travel), 1.4 mm high, two Ø0.8 pegs.
  **Sourcing is open:** JLC stock was 0 on 2026-11-08 (checked twice). An alternate search found in-stock
  side-actuated SP3T parts: Alps Alpine SSSS812201 (C160878, stock 1308; same 9.7 × 2.6 × 1.4 envelope; the
  catalogue is manufacturer-hosted), SHOU HAN MSK13C02 (C2681567, stock 28994), Hroparts K3-1310S-E1 (C145851)
  and Nidec CSS-1310TB (C2921714). None prints a per-position terminal table; each shows a one-state slider
  sketch. Under the "printed contact table" rule none is selected, and PCM13SMTR stays the documented choice
  with its zero-stock risk. SSSS812201 is the strongest fallback if that rule is relaxed or a bench continuity
  check is accepted (Q10).
- **Side switch, fitted from 2026-11-12: hanxia HX MSK13C02, LCSC C5149819**
  (`.claude/skills/component-a7-hx-msk13c02/`). The owner directed a switch that works, low price and in stock
  ("this is backside switch, so nothing special is required for this. good if it works.";
  `engineering/a7/procurement/owner-decisions.json`, switch-supply). JLC stock 4561 (Extended) on 2026-11-12.
  The Hanxia drawing served by JLC (mirror, Part No. blank) prints the contact schematic A = 1-3, B = 2-3,
  C = 3-4 with terminal 3 common, so the PCM13SMTR net mapping and mode codes are unchanged. It sits on the
  routed PCM13SMTR land; `scripts/a7_switch_fit.py` compares that land with the Hanxia recommended land on
  nominal values (`engineering/a7/electronics/switch-land-fit.json`). Lower ratings: DC 12 V 0.1 A,
  -25..+70 C (the lowest limit among the parts with a temperature fact), 10,000 cycles. The 2.7 mm body is 0.1 mm deeper, so the
  actuator tip ends 0.05 mm past the board edge (accepted in `mechanical/design-inputs.json`). The contact
  table and land fit stay physical checks (switch-fit).
- **MJ-3502 stays rejected** for 2HP: Same Sky's drawing gives body height 11.30 ± 0.2 and axis 6.90 ± 0.15.

### Historical dimensions (before #87)

These numbers describe the old study, not the regenerated current `study/report.md`.

- **3HP (14.9 mm) is feasible on paper across the width**, checked on its own: every transverse (X) margin stays
  positive at the worst corner with a standard 1.6 mm board and even untrimmed terminals. Thread engagement is a
  separate axial check that does not depend on width; it applies to 3HP exactly as to 2HP (below).
- **2HP (9.8 mm) is unresolved, not infeasible.** At nominal inputs, a jack offset 0.3–0.6 mm toward the body
  side with a ≤ 1.0 mm FR-4 controller and trimmed terminals clears everything. At the worst corner the
  terminal side goes negative. The drivers are the INFERRED axis height, the ASSUMED trim length (Q6) and the
  hole position/float (Q7). Even if the axis height were confirmed at 3.0 ± 0.1, the other assumed tolerances
  keep 2HP unresolved. With trimmed terminals no 2HP row fails at the best corner, so none is infeasible within
  the stated ranges; with untrimmed terminals every 2HP row except the 0.9 mm offset fails even at the best
  corner. A 0.9 mm offset puts the Ø8 nut on the panel edge at nominal.
- **Thread engagement:** a plain mount leaves 1.5 mm nominal and 1.1 mm worst-case exposed thread. That is
  0.85 mm engaged after the lead-in, below the 1.0 mm study criterion, so it is unresolved. Keeping the A5
  0.6 mm collar leaves 0.9 mm nominal; drop it. A front washer makes it worse. A 0.5 mm concealed rear pocket
  in the PMMA at each jack gives ≥ 1.25 mm engaged at the worst corner, so it is the option R2 should evaluate.
  It depends on Q8 and Q9.
- **Depth:** the jack body ends at Z ≤ 14.4 (14.0 in a pocket at its shallowest). That is 3.0 mm in front of the A5 optical skin
  (Z 17.4) and 5.0 mm in front of the A5 leaves (Z 19.4). A frontward controller volume and a separate rear
  optical volume are geometrically compatible at the jack positions; R2 derives the real boundary.
- **Y layout at the A5 jack positions (90 / 104), worst case** (body-edge tolerances, including the 6.1 mm far
  edge, which is calculated rather than printed, plus hole position and float): 1.5 mm between bodies, 5.5 mm
  between nuts, lower body edge at Y 110.7 against the 111.4 rail-body limit (0.7 mm margin).
