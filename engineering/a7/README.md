# engineering/a7 — A7 structural redesign (study in progress)

A7 is the structural redesign tracked in [#15](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/15):
right-angle threaded CV jacks with a controller PCB perpendicular to the front, a PCB-side ON / PGM / CV
setting switch instead of the front toggle, and a feasibility study of transparent PET FPC for the LED leaves.

**Status: design study. Not released for manufacture.** Nothing here is part approval, fit approval or an
electrical result. `engineering/a5/` stays the immutable A5 checkpoint; A7 never edits it.

## R1 findings (2026-11-08)

### Parts

- **Jack: SOFNG PJ-324M, JLCPCB/LCSC C22355739** (`evidence/component-a7-pj-324m/`). The SOFNG drawing confirms
  M6x0.5, body height 6.0 (headline 6.3), width 11.5 with the axis 5.4 from one edge and 6.1 from the other,
  body 14.2 behind a 3.5 barrel, 3.2 untrimmed terminals from the seating plane, and the terminal positions.
  It does **not** dimension the axis height (carried as INFERRED ≈3.0, band 2.6–3.4), give hole sizes, label
  pin functions, or fill in the operating temperature. JLC lists it as a wave-soldered plug-in part; stock 6209
  on 2026-11-08.
- **Pin functions are inferred, not sourced.** Pin 1 = sleeve, 2 = ring, 4 = tip, 3/5 = normals is the
  proposal (symbol convention plus terminal depth). A tip/ring swap would put CV_THRU on the contact that
  touches a TS plug's sleeve, so an incoming-part continuity test with TS and TRS plugs is required.
- **Nut:** no nut is shown or listed with C22355739. Same Sky `3.5mm-nut-e` (Ø8.0 × 1.5) and `MJ-3502-nut`
  (Ø8.0 × 1.8) are M6x0.5 dimension references only. Their fit on the SOFNG barrel is unverified.
- **Side switch: C&K PCM13SMTR, LCSC C221842** (`evidence/component-a7-pcm13smtr/`). The manufacturer table
  gives POS.1 = 1-3, POS.2 = 2-3, POS.3 = 3-4 with terminal 3 common. With 3 = GND, 1 = MODE_A, 4 = MODE_B and
  2 open, the positions give ON (0,1), PROGRAM (1,1, physical centre) and CV (1,0), as the A5 firmware expects.
  It is side-actuated (actuator projects 1.5 mm past the body edge, 1.5 mm travel), 1.4 mm high, two Ø0.8 pegs.
  **Sourcing is open:** JLC stock was 0 on 2026-11-08. Nidec CSS-1310TB (C2921714, stock 785) is the
  alternate, but its catalogue schematic does not give a per-position terminal table, so it is not selected.
- **MJ-3502 stays rejected** for 2HP: Same Sky's drawing gives body height 11.30 ± 0.2 and axis 6.90 ± 0.15.

### Dimensions (`study/report.md`)

- **3HP (14.9 mm) is feasible on paper**, checked on its own: every margin stays positive at the worst corner
  with a standard 1.6 mm board and even untrimmed terminals.
- **2HP (9.8 mm) is unresolved, not infeasible.** At nominal inputs, a jack offset 0.3–0.6 mm toward the body
  side with a ≤ 1.0 mm FR-4 controller and trimmed terminals clears everything. At the worst corner the
  terminal side goes negative. The drivers are the INFERRED axis height, the ASSUMED trim length (Q6) and the
  hole position/float (Q7). Even if the axis height were confirmed at 3.0 ± 0.1, the other assumed tolerances
  keep 2HP unresolved. A centred jack on a 1.6 mm board fails even at the best corner. A 0.9 mm offset puts
  the Ø8 nut on the panel edge.
- **Thread engagement:** a plain mount leaves 1.5 mm nominal and 1.1 mm worst-case exposed thread. That is
  0.85 mm engaged after the lead-in, below the 1.0 mm study criterion, so it is unresolved. Keeping the A5
  0.6 mm collar leaves 0.9 mm nominal; drop it. A front washer makes it worse. A 0.5 mm concealed rear pocket
  in the PMMA at each jack gives ≥ 1.25 mm engaged at the worst corner, so it is the option R2 should evaluate.
  It depends on Q8 and Q9.
- **Depth:** the jack body ends at Z ≤ 14.4 (13.9 in a pocket). That is 3.0 mm in front of the A5 optical skin
  (Z 17.4) and 5.0 mm in front of the A5 leaves (Z 19.4). A frontward controller volume and a separate rear
  optical volume are geometrically compatible at the jack positions; R2 derives the real boundary.
- **Y layout at the A5 jack positions (90 / 104):** 2.5 mm between bodies, 6.0 mm between nuts, lower body edge
  at Y 110.1 against the 111.4 rail-body limit.

### Transparent LED board

See `pet-fpc.md`. JLC's PET FPC (0.14 / 0.24 mm, up to 85%, "up to 200 °C", SMT) is captured with hashes.
The open process questions are written there. Rigid-flex is not offered, so the controller-to-film joint is a
connector. The FR-4 leaf stays the reference and fallback.
