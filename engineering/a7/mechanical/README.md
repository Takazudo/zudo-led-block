# engineering/a7/mechanical — A7 R2 digital mechanical model

**Status: provisional digital study.** Nothing here is a measured fit, a part approval or a manufacturing
release. The PJ-324M jack-axis height is INFERRED (≈3.0, band 2.6–3.4); tolerances marked ASSUMED are
unsourced. Tracked in [#17](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-led-block/issues/17) (epic #15).

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `design-inputs.json` | R2's own choices (each with a confidence label). Jack, nut, panel, PCB and tolerance inputs come only from `../study/parameters.json` (R1); switch dimensions from `../evidence/component-a7-pcm13smtr/facts.json`. |
| `layout.json`, `layout.md` | Generated by `scripts/a7_mechanical_layout.py` (pure Python): candidate sweep and selection, break-even values, Z/Y stack, per-width X placement, controller outline and keep-outs, load path, cable routes, unresolved inputs. |
| `build_a7_mechanical.py` | CadQuery 2.7.0 assembly from `layout.json`. |
| `exports/<width>/zudo_led_block_A7_<width>_assembled.step` / `.glb` | Inspectable assembly (STEP for CAD, GLB for a browser viewer). |
| `exports/<width>/view_A7_<width>_{front,transverse,side}.png` | Dimensioned views generated from the solids. |
| `exports/<width>/verification.json` | Pairwise rigid intersections, cable centreline clearances, envelope checks, optical-obstruction check, switch tool access, STEP round trip. |

## Commands

```sh
python3 scripts/a7_mechanical_layout.py            # regenerate layout.json / layout.md
python3 scripts/a7_mechanical_layout.py --check    # fail if stale
python3 engineering/a7/front_panel/build_a7_front.py       # artwork (needs CadQuery 2.7.0, ezdxf, matplotlib)
python3 engineering/a7/mechanical/build_a7_mechanical.py   # assembly, views, verification (same)
python3 -m unittest scripts/tests/test_a7_r2_mechanical.py # geometry tests; fails if the CAD export is stale
```

Run the front build before the mechanical build (the assembly imports the panel). Both read
`engineering/a5` only through imports and JSON reads; they never write there.

## Result: 2HP is held for evidence, 3HP is the modelled fallback

- **2HP primary candidate:** jack offset 0.6 mm toward the body side, 0.8 mm FR-4, trimmed leads. Chosen by a
  stated rule over R1's own margin calculation swept at 0.05 mm steps. Every nominal margin is positive
  (lead side +0.70, body side +0.70, nut edge +0.30). At the worst corner it is **UNRESOLVED**, not infeasible:
  lead side −0.33, body side −0.15. No 2HP offset/thickness passes the worst corner.
- **What closes 2HP** (one input at a time, `layout.md`): axis height ≤ 3.07 on the lead side and ≥ 2.75 on the
  body side (R1 Q1), or guaranteed trimmed protrusion ≤ 0.67 (Q6) together with an overall-height tolerance
  ≤ 0.05. The hole position tolerance alone cannot close either margin.
- **3HP fallback:** centred, 1.6 mm FR-4, untrimmed leads. **FEASIBLE ON PAPER** at the worst corner, verified
  separately in its own CAD build.
- **The controller outline is common to both widths** (Y 48.5–111.4 × Z 0.2–16.4, 1019 mm² against the
  A5 board's 554 mm²). Only its X position and thickness change, so R4 can start before the width is frozen.

## Architecture

- **Frontward controller volume / rear optical volume.** The A5 optical stack is kept: cap 1.4 + leaf 9.0 +
  skin-to-leaf 2.0 = 12.4 mm, so the optical volume is Z 17.4–29.8 within the 30 mm target. The controller ends
  at Z 16.4, 1.0 mm in front of it. The jacks need Z ≥ 14.0, leaving 2.4 mm slack. No controller, support or
  A7 harness part enters either leaf emission corridor. The remaining corridor items are the A5 leaf-mounted
  connectors and capacitors, the A5 caps and hardware, the A5 leaf link, and the A7 LED harness where it
  leaves the leaf connector.
- **Jacks:** SOFNG PJ-324M, IN at Y 86.5 and THRU at Y 101.0, 14.5 mm pitch. The A5 pitch was 14.0; it grows
  so each label sits between the jack bodies, because a label printed in front of an opaque body or a pocket
  would not light up. Both jacks seat in 0.5 mm rear pockets, the only R1 option meeting the 1.0 mm
  thread-engagement criterion (1.25 worst case). The rectangular pocket also keys the body against nut
  torque. Same Sky 3.5mm-nut-e is the dimension reference, and its fit is UNVERIFIED.
- **Load path:** insertion goes through the nut to the PMMA front, withdrawal through the body to the pocket
  floor, and torque into the pocket walls. The controller hangs on the two clamped jacks and a printed spine
  slot (Y 50–72). The solder joints carry no primary load.
- **Setting switch:** C&K PCM13SMTR at the bottom PCB edge. The actuator reaches Y 111.4 and slides along Z.
  It is reached from below with the module out of the rack, and the tool corridor is clear in both widths.
  ON / PGM / CV goes on the controller silkscreen (R4).
- **Power:** the A5 separate power adapter (keyed IDC10) is kept. A vertical IDC10 header needs 8.9 mm of X,
  but the 2HP component face offers 7.0 mm. A 50 mm PicoBlade harness runs to a header on the controller
  component face. The A5 IDC ribbon side-exit scenario still extends past the 2HP outline (carried over).
- **Harness routes (bundle model):** the controller-to-leaf SH4 route uses exactly the 50 mm finished length
  with R ≥ 2.0. The power route is 51.7 mm in 2HP and 52.7 mm in 3HP, inside the 50 ± 6 band. In 2HP the
  PicoBlade wires have only 0.9 mm of bend room above the mated plug, and the 2.3 mm tails must be cut like
  the jack leads (R2-C1).
